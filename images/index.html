The Landscape

Challenges & Opportunities

Understanding the complex dynamics of international medical travel to China

Challenges Patients Face

  • Information asymmetry & language barriers when researching Chinese hospitals
  • Navigating complex medical visa requirements and appointment scheduling
  • Lack of transparent pricing across different hospitals and treatment plans
  • Cultural differences and unfamiliarity with China's healthcare system
  • Difficulty verifying hospital accreditations and specialist credentials remotely

✓ The Opportunity

  • China has 30+ JCI-accredited hospitals offering world-class treatment
  • Advanced medical technologies at 40-60% lower cost than Western countries
  • Specialized centers of excellence in oncology, cardiology, orthopedics and more
  • Growing government support for medical tourism infrastructure
  • Established medical hubs in Shanghai, Beijing, Guangzhou and Suzhou
Kiwitech bridges this gap — making China's healthcare accessible, transparent, and reliable for international patients.
